SideStep Wizard

Hand anyone a one-click installer for your iOS app.

Point it at your app's GitHub repo once, and it builds a small installer you can send to people or link from your website. Opening it installs SideStep, gets their iPhone or iPad ready, and installs your app. No Xcode, and no developer account on their end.

Why use it

Ship your app without the App Store

Your users don't need to know what sideloading is. They open one file, follow the steps it shows, and your app is on their iPhone, kept signed and up to date from then on.

📄

One file to send

A single installer app, or a .zip to host on your site. Email it, link it, or put it on your app's Releases page.

🔄

Never out of date

The installer bundles no copy of SideStep: it downloads the latest at run time, so nobody gets a stale version.

🔗

Survives a rename

It finds your app by the repo's permanent GitHub id, with no URL shortener and nothing to register.

✅

Opens without warnings

The installer is the same signed, notarized app, just renamed, so Gatekeeper lets it straight through.

How it works

Three steps, about a minute

You need a public GitHub repo whose Releases hold your app's .ipa.

1

Point it at your repo

Enter the repo and an app name. The wizard looks up the repo's permanent GitHub id, so the installer keeps finding your app even if you rename the repo later.

2

It builds the installer

Click Create Installer and two files land on your Desktop: the installer app, to send or test, and a .zip of it to host. Tell the wizard where you'll host the .zip.

3

Copy the website button

Paste the ready-made HTML into your site. Its settings are marked at the top (INSTALLER_URL, BUTTON_CLASS, BUTTON_STYLE): use your own button class and it matches your design.

The one-click button

Try it

This button installs Magnatune (a music player) with SideStep. If you already have SideStep, it opens straight to Magnatune. If not, it downloads the Magnatune installer, which installs SideStep first and walks you through setup.

On a phone or a PC the button explains that SideStep needs a Mac, instead of doing nothing.

What your users get

Every step checked for real

Opening the installer walks them through everything, confirming each step over USB:

  1. Gets SideStep: downloads the latest notarized SideStep and installs it, or updates an older copy in place.
  2. Connects their iPhone or iPad over USB and waits for them to tap Trust.
  3. Turns on Developer Mode (iOS 16 and later), carrying on after the restart.
  4. Installs your app: SideStep shows a confirmation, they click Install, then trust the developer once in Settings ▸ General ▸ VPN & Device Management.

From then on SideStep keeps the app signed and up to date in the background, over Wi-Fi, so it behaves like a normal, permanently installed app.
